Privacy Policy

Toilet Timer · JSDev

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how JSDev ("we", "us" or "our"), the developer of Toilet Timer (the "App"), handles information when you use the App. It covers information stored on your device and information that may be processed by third-party services used for advertising and in-app purchases.

1. Who is responsible for the App

Toilet Timer is developed and published by JSDev. For privacy questions or requests, contact teamjsdev@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

The App does not require you to create an account and does not operate a JSDev user account or cloud profile. Features such as the timer, reminders, settings and personal progress tracking store information locally on your device.

Depending on the features you use, locally stored information may include:

  • Timer state, start/completion timestamps and elapsed time.
  • Personal progress entries, daily scores, mood selections and habit check-ins that you choose to enter.
  • Reminder schedules, notification preferences, sound/volume settings and widget preferences.
  • Local purchase-entitlement and compatibility information used to determine whether Pro access or legacy access is available.

These records are intended to remain on your device. We do not receive them on JSDev servers through the App, and we do not use them to create a personal profile. The App does not provide a JSDev account-based synchronization service.

Your Android device or Google account may back up some App data if device backup is enabled and supported by your system settings. Such backup is controlled by Android/Google and your device settings, not by a separate JSDev cloud service.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

Eligible users of the free version may see banner advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. Advertisements are not shown to users who have an active Toilet Timer Pro entitlement or who qualify for the App's grandfathered ad-free access.

When an ad is requested or displayed, Google and its advertising technology providers may automatically collect or receive information from the App and your device. Depending on your device, region, consent choices and Google settings, this may include:

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it.
  • Device identifiers, including advertising identifiers where available, and information used for fraud prevention or ad measurement.
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system, language, app version and network information.
  • Ad request, impression, click and interaction information, and diagnostic or performance data.

Google may use this information to deliver, measure, limit, personalize or prevent fraud in advertising, subject to its own policies and applicable settings and requirements. Whether ads are personalized or non-personalized can depend on your location, applicable consent requirements and the choices or controls available on your device.

You can review Google's explanation of how it uses data from apps that use its services in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, and review or manage relevant ad settings at Google My Ad Center. You can also read the Google Privacy Policy.

Google acts independently in relation to information it processes through AdMob. JSDev does not receive your advertising identifier or your individual ad profile from Google through the App's ordinary ad display integration.

4. In-app purchases and Google Play Billing

The App offers an optional one-time in-app purchase, Toilet Timer Pro, through Google Play Billing. The purchase is a non-consumable, one-time payment and is not a subscription. It unlocks the Pro benefits described in the App, including removal of advertisements and access to the home-screen widget for users who are not already entitled to those benefits.

Purchases are processed by Google Play. JSDev does not collect or store your credit card number, bank details or other payment credentials. Google may process information associated with your Google Play account, purchase, payment method, transaction and order in accordance with Google's terms and privacy policy. The App receives purchase-related information, such as product ownership and purchase status, so it can verify and provide the entitlement.

For more information, see Google Payments, the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

5. Notifications and device permissions

If you enable reminders or timer notifications, the App uses Android notification and scheduling features to deliver them on your device. Notification settings and reminder schedules are stored locally. The App does not send notification content to JSDev servers.

6. Data sharing and third parties

We do not sell personal information and do not share the locally stored timer or progress records with advertisers. The third-party processing described in this Policy is limited to services needed for the App's monetization and platform functionality, principally:

  • Google AdMob, which may process advertising and device information as described above.
  • Google Play Billing, which processes purchases and provides purchase status to the App.
  • Google/Android platform services, which may provide device backup, notifications, app distribution and related platform functions according to your settings and Google's policies.

These providers may process information on servers located in countries other than your own. Their processing is governed by their own privacy terms and applicable law.

7. Data retention and deletion

Information stored locally by the App generally remains on your device until you delete it through the App (where that option is available), clear the App's storage or uninstall the App. Some information may remain in an Android or Google device backup if backup is enabled. Google retains advertising and purchase-related information according to its own policies and legal obligations.

Because JSDev does not receive or maintain your local timer and progress records on its servers, we cannot directly access or delete those records remotely. For information held by Google, use the privacy and account controls provided by Google.

8. Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on your country or region, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to certain processing of personal information, or to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. For information processed directly by JSDev, contact us at teamjsdev@gmail.com. For information processed by Google, use Google's privacy tools and request channels.

You can also limit or reset your device's advertising identifier, manage ad personalization and review your Google privacy settings through the controls available for your device and region.

9. Children's privacy

Toilet Timer is a general-purpose utility and is not designed for or directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through a JSDev account or server. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, please contact us so we can review the request. Google may apply its own age-related and advertising safeguards under its policies and applicable law.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps in designing and maintaining the App, but no device, storage method or electronic transmission is completely secure. You are responsible for securing access to your device and any backups or exports you create.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its services or applicable requirements change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated date. Please review it periodically.
