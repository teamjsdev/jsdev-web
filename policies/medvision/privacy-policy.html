2. Important Summary

  • MedVision does not require You to create an account.
  • Medication records, schedules, intake/adherence history, medication photos and trusted-contact details are designed to remain on Your Device. The Application does not currently operate a developer-run cloud account or medication-data server.
  • Certain free-text medication notes are encrypted at rest using a key managed by Android Keystore. This protection does not mean every database field or file is encrypted.
  • Optional SMS alerts are sent directly from Your Device through Your mobile carrier to the contact You configured. The SMS content and related history are also stored locally by the Application.
  • Premium purchases are processed by Google Play. JSDev does not receive or store Your full payment-card details.
  • The website hosting this policy may use Google Analytics as described in Section 10. This is separate from the Application's on-device medication data.

3. Information Stored Locally by the Application

Depending on the features You use and the information You enter, MedVision may store the following information in the Application's private storage or preferences on Your Device:

  • Medication information: medication names, dose descriptions, notes, critical-medication status, selected alarm sounds and references to medication images.
  • Medication images: image files or image references You choose to add to a medication, subject to the device storage and photo/camera access You grant.
  • Schedules and reminders: scheduled times, alarm settings, snooze/interactions and related reminder state.
  • Intake and adherence records: dose status, scheduled and confirmation times, missed-dose records, adherence statistics and information used to generate PDF reports.
  • Trusted-contact details: the name, phone number and/or email address You enter, together with alert preferences and consent settings.
  • SMS history: recipient details, message text, timestamps, status and technical delivery results associated with alerts initiated by the Application.
  • Application preferences: settings such as sound, grace period, notification behavior and Premium entitlement cache.

This information is used on the Device to provide reminders, record medication activity, calculate statistics, generate reports and operate the optional trusted-contact features. JSDev does not currently receive these medication records or contact details on a developer-operated server.

4. Encryption and Security

MedVision encrypts selected free-text medication notes at rest using cryptographic operations backed by Android Keystore. Other data fields, including some medication details, schedules, contact information and local history, are not necessarily encrypted individually by this mechanism. Do not interpret the presence of encryption for selected fields as a guarantee that the entire Device or database is encrypted.

We use the Android application sandbox and platform security facilities. However, no device, storage system or security measure can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Your Device lock, operating-system updates, protection against unauthorized access and physical security are important. A compromised or rooted Device may weaken these protections.

5. Camera, Photos and Device Permissions

If You choose to add medication photographs, the Application may use the camera or access an image selected from Your Device, depending on the action You take and the Android permission or system picker involved. These images are used to display and organize medication information. They are stored or referenced locally and are not uploaded by MedVision to a JSDev server.

The Application may request notification, exact-alarm, full-screen-intent, wake-lock-related system capabilities, vibration, telephony/SMS and other Android permissions needed for features You choose to use. Android controls these permissions and special accesses. You can review or revoke permissions in Device settings; doing so may disable the related feature.

6. Optional Trusted-Contact Alerts (SMS and Email)

When You configure a trusted contact, the contact details You enter are stored locally. You are responsible for ensuring that You have permission to provide and use the contact's information and that the contact understands the purpose of the alerts.

SMS

If You explicitly enable automatic SMS alerts, accept the in-app notice about possible carrier charges and grant Android's SMS permission, MedVision may send an SMS from Your Device when a medication marked as critical is not confirmed within the configured grace period. The SMS may contain the medication name, scheduled time and reminder-status information. The message is transmitted by Your mobile carrier to the phone number You supplied. It does not pass through a JSDev messaging server.

Your mobile operator and the recipient's operator may process the phone number, message content, routing and delivery information. Carrier charges may apply. JSDev does not control the carrier, guarantee delivery or know whether the recipient reads or acts on the message. The Application stores a local history of SMS requests and their reported status.

Email

If You select an email alert option, MedVision may open an email application or chooser with recipient and message details. The email is handled by the external email application and provider. Their privacy policy and terms apply. JSDev does not operate the email service and does not receive a copy of the email through a MedVision server.

You can disable trusted-contact alerts in the Application. You can also revoke the relevant Android permission or remove/update the contact details in the Application.

7. Google Play Billing and Premium Purchases

MedVision offers an optional Premium package through Google Play as a one-time in-app purchase, not a recurring subscription. The Application uses Google Play Billing to retrieve available product information and check whether the Google account currently has an eligible purchase. It stores a local Premium entitlement flag so the interface can reflect the purchase state.

Google Play processes the transaction and may collect or process information such as Google account identifiers, purchase history, transaction identifiers, payment method details, billing country, tax and fraud-prevention information under Google's own privacy terms. JSDev does not receive or store Your full payment-card number or payment credentials through the Application. We receive the purchase state and product information necessary to activate the purchased feature package; the Application does not send medication records or trusted-contact details to Google Play as part of the billing flow.

For information about Google's handling of purchase and payment data, review Google's Privacy Policy and the applicable Google Play terms. Billing support and refund requests are generally handled through Google Play, subject to applicable law.

8. Information We Do Not Collect Through the Application

Based on the current Application implementation, MedVision does not require a user account, does not operate a developer-run cloud database for medication records, and does not send medication names, doses, notes, photos, schedules, trusted-contact details or SMS history to JSDev servers. The Application does not include in-app advertising or a developer-operated marketing profile.

This statement describes the current version of the Application. If future versions introduce analytics, crash reporting, cloud synchronization, account services or other data processing, this Policy will be updated before or when those features are introduced, and any required in-app notices or choices will be provided.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored by MedVision remains on Your Device until You delete it within the Application, clear the Application's storage, or uninstall the Application, subject to Android's handling of device backups and residual storage. You can remove or edit medication and contact information through the relevant Application features. Deleting local application data or uninstalling the Application may permanently remove records and Premium's local entitlement cache; an eligible Google Play purchase can generally be checked again using the same Google account.

JSDev does not maintain a remote copy of the local medication or trusted-contact records and therefore cannot retrieve, edit or directly erase those records on Your Device. If You believe JSDev has received personal information directly, or You have a privacy request about billing-related information available to Us, contact teamjsdev@gmail.com. We will respond in accordance with applicable law. Google Play and mobile/email providers retain information under their own policies and retention practices.
