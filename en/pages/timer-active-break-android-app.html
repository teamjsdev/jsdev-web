Active Break Timer – Break Reminder App for Studying and Working

What is Active Break Timer?

Active Break Timer is an Android app designed to help reduce sedentary habits during studying, remote work or long hours in front of a screen. It uses scheduled reminders to help you drink water, move and take healthy breaks throughout the day.

Main Features

  • Automatic break timers
  • Water intake reminders
  • Movement reminders
  • Custom timer
  • Dark mode
  • Simple and reliable notifications

Ideal for students, remote workers and anyone who wants to build healthier habits during long periods of sitting.

Why take active breaks during the day?

Spending many hours sitting can affect posture, increase fatigue and reduce concentration. Active breaks help keep your body moving and support overall wellness.

Taking short breaks while studying or working can also improve productivity and reduce physical discomfort related to prolonged sitting.

Active Break Timer helps you create healthier habits through automatic reminders for movement and hydration.

Benefits of active breaks

  • Reduces sedentary habits
  • Improves posture
  • Supports hydration
  • Helps reduce mental fatigue
  • Improves productivity

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I take an active break?

Many specialists recommend taking short breaks every 30 to 60 minutes when sitting for long periods.

Do active breaks improve productivity?

Yes. Taking short breaks can help maintain focus and reduce fatigue.

Is the app useful for studying and working?

Yes. Active Break Timer was designed to support long study sessions, home office routines and desk work.
