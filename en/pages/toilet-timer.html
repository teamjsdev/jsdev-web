How Long Should You Sit on the Toilet?

Health professionals often recommend avoiding long periods of sitting on the toilet. Spending too much time can increase pressure in the rectal veins and may contribute to issues such as hemorrhoids.

Many specialists suggest limiting bathroom time to about 5 minutes. A short and relaxed routine helps maintain healthy bowel habits.
