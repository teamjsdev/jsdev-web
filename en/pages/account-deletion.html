Account & Data Deletion Request

How to request account deletion?

If you wish to delete your HotCrave account and all associated personal data without needing to reinstall the app, you can submit a request via email by following these steps:

  1. Click the button below or email our support address.
  2. Make sure to send the request from the same email address registered to your HotCrave account.
  3. Use the email subject line: "Account Deletion Request - HotCrave".
Request Account Deletion

What data will be deleted?

Upon processing your request, the following data will be permanently removed from our servers:

User Profile & Credentials Email & Account ID Saved Places & History App Preferences

Processing time & Data retention

Once received, your account and associated data will be fully deleted within 48 to 72 hours. teamJSDev does not retain any additional user data after the deletion is confirmed.
