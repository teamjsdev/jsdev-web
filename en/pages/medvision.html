MedVision – Smart Medication Reminder & Pill Alarm App

What is MedVision?

MedVision is an Android medication reminder and pill tracking app designed to help individuals and families manage daily treatments with confidence. Keeping track of multiple medications, pills, and schedules can be challenging, so MedVision provides a simple and reliable way to organize reminders, confirm doses, and monitor medication routines.

You can create personalized medication alarms, record each dose, and automatically notify a trusted contact by SMS if an important dose is not confirmed within the time window you choose. MedVision is especially useful for older adults, caregivers, and anyone who wants extra peace of mind when managing medications.

Key Features & Benefits

Custom medication reminders: Set personalized pill alarms with medication names, schedules, and dosage instructions.
Automatic SMS caregiver alerts: Send text message alerts to a trusted contact when a critical dose is not confirmed.
Photo-based pill identification: Add pictures of pill boxes, blister packs, or tablets for quick visual recognition.
Smart dose tracking: Choose which medications require strict monitoring and which only need a standard reminder.
Flexible grace periods: Define how long MedVision should wait before sending a missed-dose alert.
Voice medication alarms: Hear the medication name and dosage when the alarm sounds, ideal for seniors and visually impaired users.
100% local storage: Your medication data, schedules, and photos stay on your Android device for maximum privacy.

How the Automatic Medication Monitoring Works

  1. Set up your medication: Add the medicine and choose reminder times.
  2. Enable monitoring: Activate additional tracking for important doses.
  3. Choose a grace period: Select how long to wait before sending an alert.
  4. Automatic action: If the dose is not confirmed in time, MedVision can send an SMS alert to the selected trusted contact.
Privacy First: MedVision does not require online accounts or cloud storage. You decide which medications are monitored and who receives alerts, keeping full control of your personal information.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I manage multiple medications?

Yes. You can add multiple medications with different doses, schedules, reminders, and monitoring settings.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Medication reminders and alarms work locally on your Android device. SMS alerts use your phone's mobile network.

Can I disable SMS alerts for specific medications?

Yes. You can decide which medications trigger caregiver SMS alerts and which only show standard reminders.

What happens if I forget to confirm a dose?

If the configured grace period ends without confirmation, MedVision can send a missed-dose SMS alert to your chosen trusted contact.

Important Notice

MedVision is a medication reminder and treatment organization tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnoses, treatment recommendations, or emergency response services. Always consult your doctor or pharmacist before making changes to your medications or treatment plan.
