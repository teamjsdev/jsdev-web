Gym Apps

An app that helps your gym offer a differentiated service to its members.

Turn your members’ phones into their best workout companion.

GymLoad allows users to track workouts, weights used, sets, repetitions, machine photos, and progress statistics, all from a simple and intuitive Android app.


The app does not replace your gym’s current workflow. It complements existing training and improves the member experience without creating extra work for trainers or front-desk staff.



What benefits does your gym get?

📈 Higher member retention

Members can see their progress and track every workout, which increases motivation to keep attending.

💪 More autonomy

Each member can remember machine settings, weights, and notes without constantly relying on a trainer.

⚡ No changes to your current workflow

It does not change your existing routines or require staff training. It simply adds value to your service.

🚀 A growing platform

GymLoad continues to evolve and may include exclusive features adapted to each gym.
