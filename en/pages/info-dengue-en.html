InfoDengue – Learn About Dengue With Interactive Games

What is InfoDengue?

InfoDengue is a free educational Android app designed to help people learn about dengue in a simple and engaging way. Through interactive games, quizzes and visual content, the app explains how dengue spreads, its symptoms, and the most effective prevention methods.

  • Modes of transmission
  • Effective prevention strategies
