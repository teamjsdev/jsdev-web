What is BodyLog?

BodyLog is a simple and practical Android application designed to help you organize your meal logs, weight tracking, and wellness records all in one place.

Whether you want to control your caloric intake, track weight trends, or keep a clear history of your progress, BodyLog offers a fast and structured way to log your daily information.

The app allows you to log meals by time of day, calculate indicator estimates like BMI, BMR, and TDEE, and view your energy balance over time. All data is stored locally on your device to keep your personal information completely under your control.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Does BodyLog help with weight loss?

How accurate are the BMI, BMR, and TDEE calculations?

BodyLog uses widely recognized formulas to provide indicative estimates. Each calculation carries a different level of precision:

  • BMI (Body Mass Index): The mathematical calculation is exact because it uses your entered weight and height (weight / height²). However, its interpretation does not always reflect real body composition. For example, an individual with high muscle mass may present a high BMI without excess body fat.
  • BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate): Estimated through recognized equations such as Mifflin-St Jeor or Harris-Benedict. These formulas are based on population studies and offer a useful approximation, though actual metabolic rates vary by genetics, muscle mass, age, hormonal status, and individual factors.
  • TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure): Calculated from BMR and your selected physical activity level. Two people with identical weight and height may have very different energy needs due to training intensity, occupation, sleep quality, stress, recovery, and daily habits.

For these reasons, results provided by BodyLog should be understood as indicative estimates for personal tracking and not as a medical or nutritional assessment.

Does BodyLog help track a calorie deficit?

Yes. The app lets you log consumed calories and compare them against estimated energy expenditure, helping you visualize whether you are in a calorie deficit, maintenance, or surplus.

Can I export my logs?

Yes. You can export your history with timestamps to keep or analyze your data elsewhere.
